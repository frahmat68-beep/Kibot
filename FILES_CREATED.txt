QUESTIONS & ANSWERS
================================================================================

Q: How do I know the whitelist is working?
A: Use getPairWhitelistManager().getSummary() to see:
   - Hard whitelist count (always 3: STO, DRX, D)
   - Dynamic whitelist count (pairs that proved themselves)
   - Probationary count (pairs in testing period)
   - Blacklist count (proven losers)

Q: How do I know capital allocation is working?
A: Use getCapitalAllocator().getStatus() to see:
   - Current stable capital (target: Rp33,200)
   - Current aggressive capital (target: Rp8,300)
   - Drift percentage (should stay < 5%)
   - Rebalance count (should be low under normal trading)

Q: What happens if a pair gets blacklisted?
A: evaluateBuyOrder() returns HARD VETO with reason "PAIR_BLACKLISTED"
   The pair must accumulate 20+ trades at 65%+ winrate to be whitelisted again

Q: What happens if capital drifts > 5%?
A: Auto-rebalance triggers when depositProfit() is called
   Capital rebalances back to 70/30 split

Q: How do I test locally?
A: Run: ./gradlew :packages:core:test --tests "HybridStrategyTests"
   All 12 tests should pass with detailed output
